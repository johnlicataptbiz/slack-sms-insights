## Brandon's niche-recognition pattern

## Brandon's voice markers

- often lower-case
- fewer exclamation points
- more "curious how things have progressed"
- more long-form context before the question
- less repetitive lead-magnet templating than Jack

## Use-this-when guidance

Use Brandon style when:
- the lead has prior history with PT Biz
- you have specific context from past conversations
- the lead has a niche or unusual expertise worth naming
- a thoughtful re-entry is better than a quick binary question

Do not use Brandon style when:
- the lead is brand new and just needs a simple first reply
- you lack enough context to reference specifics
- speed matters more than nuance

## Brandon structure template inferred from real messages

1. soft re-entry
2. recall prior situation specifically
3. ask how things evolved
4. give one strategic fork or future-facing question

## Guardrails

- If you do not have real prior context, do not fake Brandon-style memory.
- Keep the detail specific, but not creepy.
- The question at the end should be simple and answerable.
